6 Golden Rules of Phone Protection

Lock Your WhatsApp with 2-Step Verification
Go to Settings > Account > Two-Step Verification > Enable. Set a 6-digit PIN so hackers cannot steal your number via SIM swap or SMS intercept.
Beware of Unknown APK File Downloads
Third-party APKs downloaded from unauthorized websites often embed trojan keyloggers that transmit banking pins and photos to remote hacker servers.
Avoid Public Unsecured Wi-Fi Networks
Hackers set up "Man-in-the-Middle" rogue Wi-Fi hotspots at public places. Never log into mobile banking or sensitive apps while connected to unknown open Wi-Fi.
Enable SIM Card Lock Pin
If your phone is stolen, thieves can put your SIM into another device to receive bank OTP codes. Lock your SIM card with a custom PIN under SIM Security settings.
